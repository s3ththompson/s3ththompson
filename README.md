I am a designer and software engineer in New York. My work engages with spatial representation, computational image-making, and tools for creative expression.

I am the founder of A Lot of Moving Parts, a research studio for interactive 3d visualization. I am also an adjunct assistant professor at Columbia University GSAPP where I teach in the Computational Design Practices graduate degree program. Previously, I worked at Notion and Google, and served as the digital director of the New York Review of Architecture.
